# The PAYE Masterclass: Understanding Your Payslip

For most people, the payslip is a mystery of codes and deductions. This masterclass strips away the jargon and explains exactly how your salary is transformed into take-home pay.

## 1. The Tax Code Secret
Your tax code (e.g., 1257L) tells your employer how much tax-free income you are entitled to. 
- **1257** represents your £12,570 Personal Allowance.
- **L** means you are entitled to the standard personal allowance.

## 2. National Insurance (NI)
National Insurance is a "secondary tax" that funds state benefits and the NHS. For employees, this is deducted via **Class 1 NI**. 

## 3. Student Loan Repayments
Student loans are calculated on your gross pay above a specific threshold. 
- **Plan 1**: Repayments start at £24,990.
- **Plan 2**: Repayments start at £27,295.
- **Plan 4**: Repayments start at £31,395.

## 4. Pension Relief
When you contribute to a workplace pension, you receive tax relief.
- **Net Pay Arrangement**: Taken before tax, so you get full relief immediately.
- **Relief at Source**: Taken after tax, and the provider claims back 20% from HMRC.
