# The Ultimate Self-Assessment Checklist (2024/25)

Filing your Self-Assessment shouldn't be a source of stress. Whether you are a Sole Trader, a Landlord, or a High-Earner, this checklist ensures you have everything ready for a smooth submission to HMRC.

## 1. Essential Information
- [ ] **UTR Number**: Your 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference.
- [ ] **National Insurance Number**: Found on your payslip or P60.
- [ ] **Government Gateway ID**: Ensure your password hasn't expired.

## 2. Income Records
- [ ] **P60 / P45**: For any employment income during the tax year.
- [ ] **Self-Employment Profit**: Total sales minus allowable expenses.
- [ ] **Property Income**: Rental income and Section 24 mortgage interest records.
- [ ] **Dividend Certificates**: For any income extracted from a Limited Company.
- [ ] **Interest Statements**: From banks and building societies.

## 3. Tax Reliefs & Deductions
- [ ] **Pension Contributions**: Records of any personal pension payments.
- [ ] **Charitable Giving**: Gift Aid declarations.
- [ ] **Business Expenses**: Receipts for tools, travel, and office costs.
