# The Personal Allowance Masterclass (2025/26)

The Personal Allowance is the foundation of the UK tax system. For the 2025/26 tax year, most individuals have a tax-free allowance of **£12,570**. However, once your income crosses certain thresholds, this "free" bucket of income begins to disappear.

In this masterclass, we break down exactly how the allowance works, when it tapers, and how you can restore it using pension contributions.

## Key Thresholds to Watch

1. **Standard Allowance (£12,570)**: What you get if you earn below £100,000.
2. **The £100,000 Tipping Point**: For every £2 you earn above £100,000, you lose £1 of your personal allowance.
3. **The 60% Tax Trap**: Because you are losing your allowance while paying 40% tax, the effective tax rate between £100k and £125,140 is a staggering **60%**.

## How to Restore Your Allowance

If your "Adjusted Net Income" is between £100k and £125k, you can "buy back" your personal allowance by making pension contributions or gift aid donations.

* **Example**: If you earn £110,000 and contribute £10,000 to your pension, your Adjusted Net Income drops back to £100,000, and your full £12,570 allowance is restored.
