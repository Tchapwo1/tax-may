Key deadlines — don't miss them

HMRC issues automatic penalties for late filing and late payment. Missing the deadline by even one day triggers a £100 fine.

31
Jan 2026

Online filing — 2024–25 tax year

Submit your Self Assessment return online and pay any tax due. Late penalty: £100 immediately, increasing after 3 months.

31
Oct 2025

Paper filing — 2024–25 tax year

If filing a paper return, the deadline is 31 October — three months earlier than the online deadline.

31
Jan 2026

Payments on account

First payment on account for 2025–26 is due alongside your 2024–25 balancing payment on 31 January 2026.

60
days CGT

CGT property report — separate deadline

Capital gains on UK residential property must be reported and paid within 60 days of completion. This is separate from Self Assessment.
