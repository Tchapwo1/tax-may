What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and actions, and help us understand how visitors use our site. Some cookies are essential for the site to function; others are optional and require your consent under UK law (PECR).

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how our site is used. Analytics cookies are only set after you give consent via our cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking "Cookie settings" in the page footer.

Analytics data is aggregated and anonymous. We cannot identify you from analytics data. Google may process this data in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses.

How to control cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Our cookie banner — shown on your first visit. Accept or decline optional cookies.
  • Cookie settings link — available in the footer at any time to update your preferences.
  • Browser settings — all modern browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note this may affect site functionality.
  • Google Analytics opt-out — install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Calculator tools and local storage

Our calculators may use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to temporarily remember your calculation inputs within a session. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is cleared when you close your browser or clear your site data.
